Why Cook County data

For these searches, Keyword Planner shows almost no data at the level of small cities like Oak Lawn. We used Cook County to measure demand and estimated our area from it.

Emergency and extraction searches

Google does not share Keyword Planner data for health-sensitive topics such as emergency dentist, tooth extraction and wisdom teeth removal. People search for these every day; Google simply keeps the numbers private. We will see real volume once the campaign runs.
